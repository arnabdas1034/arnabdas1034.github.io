Vision

where this is going

Automation for businesses that never got any

Most small businesses in India still run on work that software solved years ago. Lead lists built by hand. Quotes retyped one at a time. Follow-ups that only happen if somebody remembers. I want to build the practice that closes that gap, starting with freelance work once my internship ends and growing it into something of my own.

Websites are how I plan to get in the door, because a website is what people already know to ask for. The real work sits behind it. I have built both halves of a sales pipeline separately: a scraper that produces verified contact lists, and an outreach engine that writes a personalised opener for every lead. Joining them into one thing a business can actually buy is the next step.

After that, voice. A lot of the calls a small shop makes are the same call every time. Confirming an appointment. Chasing a payment. Following up on a quote nobody replied to. An AI agent can take those, and the person who was making them gets their afternoon back.

Where I want this to end up is a team you can rent. A sales side that finds customers, writes to them and calls them. A dev side that builds and maintains whatever the business needs to run. The shops I want to work with have three or four staff, not three hundred. Automation there does not take anyone's job. It gives one person back the two hours a day they were losing to copy and paste, and it saves the owner money they were never going to spend on hiring anyway.
